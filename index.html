Pattern recognition

Recurring methods

01

Borrowed identity

Operators pose as locals, activists, journalists, or community admins so the audience misreads the source.

02

Artificial scale

Fake accounts reply, like, repost, and coordinate timing to simulate momentum before real users arrive.

03

Platform migration

When detection improves, networks push content through smaller forums, copied sites, or repurposed pages.

04

Issue hijacking

The most effective content often attaches itself to real grievances instead of inventing new ones.

Editorial method

What counts as documented

Every case must be supported by a court record, law-enforcement action, platform investigation, or published forensic research. Evidence grades describe the public record, not the severity or effectiveness of the campaign.

Grade A
Court record or official law-enforcement action.
Grade B
Platform attribution supported by technical or independent research.
Grade C
Platform attribution with limited public technical detail.

Built as a public-interest casebook. Claims should be updated when new disclosures or court records become available.
